🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues
  • CORS Error: Check backend/config/cors.php and sanctum.php
  • 404 Not Found: Backend server not running or wrong URL
  • Invalid State: Try again, state expires after 10 minutes
  • No Token: Check backend logs for errors
  • Redirect Loop: Clear cookies and try again
